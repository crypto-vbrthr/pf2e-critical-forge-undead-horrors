# Changelog

## 0.1.0
- Initial Undead Horrors extension scaffold.
- Registers through the Critical Forge extension controller.
- Adds five Undead Critical Hit integration-test cards.
- Adds German and English localization.
- Exercises `sourceTraits`, `excludedSourceTraits`, and `excludedTargetTraits` matching.

## 0.1.1
- Added world settings for independently enabling or disabling each Undead Horrors card pack.
- Split the first test content into the dedicated **Undead Critical Hits** pack.
- Pack registration now synchronizes live with settings through the Critical Forge extension API.
- Reserved settings and pack IDs for **Undead Fumbles** and **Critical Hits Against Undead**.
